Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What types of vehicles do you service?

We service most makes and models, including cars, SUVs, and light trucks. If you have a specific question about your vehicle, please contact us!

How can I book an appointment?

You can call us directly, email us, or use the booking form on our Contact Us page to schedule your appointment. If we’re fully booked, you can fill out the Appointment Form, and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

Do you offer any warranty on your repairs?

Yes! We stand by our work with a 3-month service warranty on most repairs. Terms and conditions apply.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers, and most mobile payment services (e.g., POS, USSD). Please let us know your preferred payment method when booking.

How long will my repair take?

Repair times vary depending on the type of service needed. Most routine maintenance can be completed the same day, while major repairs may take longer. We’ll always give you an estimated timeline upfront.

Can I wait while my car is being serviced?

Yes! We have a waiting area where you can relax while your car is being worked on. For longer repairs, we can give you updates so you can go about your day and return when your car is ready.

Legal & Practicalities

What is your Privacy Policy?

We take your privacy seriously. We only collect the information necessary to provide our services (like name, contact details, and vehicle information) and never share your data without your consent. For more details, please request our full Privacy Policy document.

What are your Terms of Service?

Our Terms of Service cover things like service scope, payment terms, warranty coverage, and dispute resolution. We’re happy to provide a full copy on request to ensure you’re informed before we begin any work on your vehicle.
