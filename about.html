About Precision Auto Repairs

Hi there, my name is Precision, and I’m a proud Nigerian mechanic with over five years of hands-on experience. I’ve always had a deep passion for cars — the way they sound, the way they move, and the stories they carry. Over the years, I’ve built a reliable team of skilled mechanics who share my commitment to quality and attention to detail. Together, we treat every car like it’s our own, ensuring no job is too big or too small. With my team, we’re able to handle multiple projects efficiently without compromising on the personal touch and care that make us different. That means you can trust us to get the job done right and on time, every time.
